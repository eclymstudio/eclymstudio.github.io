Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ECLYM Studio ("ECLYM Studio", "we", "our", or "us") handles information in mobile applications, games, websites, and other digital services published or operated by ECLYM Studio.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to ECLYM Studio applications and digital services that link to this page. Individual products may use different features or third-party services, so not every item described below necessarily applies to every product.

2. Information We Process

Many ECLYM Studio applications can be used without creating an ECLYM Studio account or directly providing personal information to us. Depending on the product and features used, information may nevertheless be processed automatically by the application, the operating system, an app store, or third-party service providers.

This may include information such as:

  • device and operating-system information;
  • application version, language, and general usage or diagnostic information;
  • network and technical information used to deliver or secure services;
  • advertising identifiers, where permitted by the operating system and applicable law;
  • purchase or entitlement information needed to provide in-app purchases or restore purchases.

3. Advertising

Some ECLYM Studio applications display advertising. Depending on the individual application, we may use one or more third-party advertising networks, advertising platforms, or mediation partners to provide, personalize where permitted, measure, secure, and improve advertisements.

Advertising providers used by a particular application may include Google AdMob, Yandex Mobile Ads / Yandex Advertising Network, and other advertising or mediation partners integrated into that application. Not every advertising provider listed or described in this Policy is used by every ECLYM Studio product.

Depending on the advertising provider, device settings, consent choices, location, and applicable law, advertising services may process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, advertising interactions, diagnostic or performance information, and other technical information. This information may be used for purposes including ad delivery, frequency management, measurement, analytics, fraud and abuse prevention, and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

Users can learn more about the privacy practices of major advertising providers that may be used in ECLYM Studio applications:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Privacy Policy

Additional advertising or mediation providers may be used in individual applications. Where applicable, those providers process information under their own terms, privacy policies, and data-protection obligations.

ECLYM Studio does not ask users to click advertisements and does not intentionally generate artificial ad impressions or clicks.

4. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, we or our advertising providers may request consent, provide privacy choices, or limit certain forms of data processing before using information for advertising purposes. These requirements may vary by jurisdiction and by the advertising provider used in a particular application.

Users in regions with applicable privacy laws, including the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom where relevant, may be provided with controls relating to personal-data processing, personalized advertising, or consent withdrawal.

On supported devices, users may also limit, reset, or delete advertising identifiers, restrict ad personalization, or manage privacy permissions through their device, operating-system, or platform settings.

ECLYM Studio products may rely on third-party platforms or services to provide functionality. Depending on the app, these may include app-store distribution, billing, crash reporting, analytics, advertising, mediation, fraud prevention, or other platform services.

These third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Examples may include Google Play services and Google Play Billing on Android devices, Google AdMob, Yandex Mobile Ads, and other advertising or mediation providers used by a particular application.

The specific combination of third-party services may differ from one ECLYM Studio application to another and may change over time as products, technical requirements, or service providers change.

When an application offers an in-app purchase, payment processing is normally handled by the applicable app store or payment platform. ECLYM Studio does not receive users' full payment-card numbers from the app store. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, or entitlement information needed to provide or restore purchased features.

We take children's privacy seriously. Applications intended for children, or applications that may be used by children, are configured and operated in accordance with applicable platform rules and privacy laws. Where required, advertising or data-processing features may be restricted for users identified as children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to ECLYM Studio in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

ECLYM Studio seeks to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security. Information processed independently by third-party providers is subject to those providers' retention and security practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information we control.
