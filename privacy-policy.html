Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ECLYM Studio ("ECLYM Studio", "we", "our", or "us") handles information in mobile applications, games, websites, and other digital services published or operated by ECLYM Studio.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to ECLYM Studio applications and digital services that link to this page. Individual products may use different features or third-party services, so not every item described below necessarily applies to every product.

2. Information We Process

Many ECLYM Studio applications can be used without creating an ECLYM Studio account or directly providing personal information to us. Depending on the product and features used, information may nevertheless be processed automatically by the application, the operating system, an app store, or third-party service providers.

This may include information such as:

  • device and operating-system information;
  • application version, language, and general usage or diagnostic information;
  • network and technical information used to deliver or secure services;
  • advertising identifiers, where permitted by the operating system and applicable law;
  • purchase or entitlement information needed to provide in-app purchases or restore purchases.

3. Advertising

Some ECLYM Studio applications display advertising. We may use Yandex Mobile Ads / Yandex Advertising Network ("Yandex") to provide and measure advertisements.

Depending on the user's device settings, consent choices, location, and applicable law, Yandex and its advertising partners may process technical information and advertising identifiers for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

Users can learn more about Yandex's privacy practices in the Yandex Privacy Policy.

ECLYM Studio does not ask users to click advertisements and does not intentionally generate artificial ad impressions or clicks.

4. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, we or our advertising providers may request consent before processing data for advertising purposes. Users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland may be provided with controls relating to personal-data processing and advertising.

On supported devices, users may also limit or reset advertising identifiers, restrict ad personalization, or manage privacy permissions through their device or operating-system settings.

5. Third-Party Services

ECLYM Studio products may rely on third-party platforms or services to provide functionality. Depending on the app, these may include app-store distribution, billing, crash reporting, analytics, advertising, or other platform services.

These third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Examples may include Google Play services and Google Play Billing on Android devices, as well as Yandex Mobile Ads in advertising-supported applications.

6. In-App Purchases

When an application offers an in-app purchase, payment processing is normally handled by the applicable app store or payment platform. ECLYM Studio does not receive users' full payment-card numbers from the app store. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, or entitlement information needed to provide or restore purchased features.

7. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. Applications intended for children, or applications that may be used by children, are configured and operated in accordance with applicable platform rules and privacy laws. Where required, advertising or data-processing features may be restricted for users identified as children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to ECLYM Studio in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

8. Data Retention and Security

ECLYM Studio seeks to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security. Information processed independently by third-party providers is subject to those providers' retention and security practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information we control.

9. International Processing

ECLYM Studio applications are distributed internationally. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where a user lives. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, providers are responsible for implementing appropriate safeguards for their processing activities.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Some information is controlled directly by third-party providers; requests relating to that information may need to be made directly to the relevant provider.
